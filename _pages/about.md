**Linwei Zhu**, Associate Professor, Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

His research focuses on multimedia signal processing, image/video coding and decoding, covering machine vision, underwater image/video enhancement and compression, 3D video coding and processing, virtual view rendering, static/dynamic point cloud enhancement and compression, etc.

He has served as the principal investigator for the Young Scientists Fund of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, Special Grant and General Grant of the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, General Program of the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province, General Program of the Natural Science Foundation of Shenzhen, and other research projects. As first or corresponding author, he has published multiple SCI papers in top-tier journals including IEEE T-IP, IEEE T-CSVT and IEEE T-MM. He has been granted more than ten Chinese and US invention patents.
