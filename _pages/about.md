# 朱林卫 | Linwei Zhu
副研究员，中国科学院深圳先进技术研究院

长期从事多媒体信号处理、图像与视频编解码研究，研究方向包括机器视觉、水下图像/视频增强与压缩、3D视频编码与虚拟视点渲染、静态/动态点云增强与压缩、视觉感知质量评价、光场编码与质量评价等。

主持国家自然科学基金青年项目、中国博士后科学基金特别资助与面上项目、广东省自然科学基金面上项目、深圳市自然科学基金面上项目等多项课题。以第一/通讯作者在 IEEE T-IP、IEEE T-CSVT、IEEE T-MM 等国际知名期刊发表多篇SCI论文，获授权中国及美国发明专利十余项。

# Linwei Zhu
Associate Researcher, Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), Chinese Academy of Sciences

My research focuses on multimedia signal processing and image/video coding. My interests include machine vision, underwater image/video enhancement and compression, 3D video coding and virtual view synthesis, static/dynamic point cloud enhancement and compression, perceptual visual quality assessment, and light-field coding and quality evaluation.

I have been the principal investigator of the Youth Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, Special and General Grants of the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation, General Program of Guangdong Natural Science Foundation, General Program of Shenzhen Natural Science Foundation, etc. I have published multiple SCI papers as first/corresponding author in top journals including IEEE T-IP, IEEE T-CSVT, and IEEE T-MM. I hold more than ten granted Chinese and US patents.
